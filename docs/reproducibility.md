# Reproducibility protocol

## What is guaranteed

| Claim | How it is checked |
|---|---|
| Every table and figure of the manuscript is regenerated by code in this repository | `python -m reproduce build` (all 24 artifacts) |
| Every number printed in the manuscript's tables matches its recorded source, or the difference is documented | `make verify-paper`; differences in `reproduce/known_deviations.csv` |
| The recorded sources are the files the paper was built from | `evidence/MANIFEST.sha256`; source indexes in `reproduce/sources/` |
| The experiments behind each table can be re-run with the paper's configuration and protocol | `reproduce/jobs/<artifact>.job` (build stage verifies the fresh numbers) |
| The public datasets, the manuscript and the certified outputs are unchanged | `make verify-data`, `make verify-certified` |

Two results cannot currently be rebuilt from recorded files: Table 6(a) and
Table 17.  Their builders and jobs exist; the result files behind the printed
values were not preserved.  `python -m reproduce list` shows the status of
every artifact.

## Evaluation protocol

* **Splits.** 85/5/10 edge split.  Fixed split (seed 42) for Tables 4, 7, 8, 11
  and 20, with 10 training seeds (42-51); deterministic heuristics are run
  once.  Tables 15 and 16 use the split-resampled protocol: seed *s* trains on
  split *s* (0-9).  Brightkite always uses its proxy-temporal split
  (`data/SNAP/brightkite/temporal_split.pt`).
* **Summaries.** Mean ± population SD over runs (fixed split); paired mean
  difference ± 95% t half-width with Holm correction within the families of
  Table 14 (split-resampled).
* **Equality Gain.** Degree EG counts target-indegree recommendation events
  (result marker `legacy_indegree_reconstructed`); PageRank and Closeness EG use
  the simple undirected graph of distinct pairs.  Closeness on Pokec-n and
  Brightkite uses the exact generalized closeness of
  `metrics/closeness_exact_v1.py` (Tables 4, 7, 11, 15; Figure 3); Table 20 and
  the stratified analyses retain the evaluation of their original campaigns.
* **Configurations.** `configs/best_configs.csv` (SHA-256
  `44ea8142fa6e953dc2c79fbe3d5b6ae5e4eb77ad08a3a72636db31942e9c4ade`), selected
  on validation edges with the two-stage Pareto/ASF rule of Appendix A.2.  The
  fusion control uses the separately selected β in Tables 7 and 15(b) and
  CAFE-In's β in Tables 6 and 11.
* **Pokec-n baselines.** The nine learned baselines of Table 4 are means over
  8 runs (seeds 42-47, 50-51).

## Re-running on new hardware

GPU training is not bit-reproducible across devices and library versions.
Verify reruns with a tolerance (`TOLERANCE`, in printed units).  The Resource
Allocation heuristic pads short candidate lists with random nodes, so its EG
values depend on the random-number stream even though its precision does not.

## Environments

* `environment-smoke.yml`: CPU environment for the smoke test, the table
  builders and the tests.
* `environment.yml`: the full GPU environment of the experiments.

## Certified release outputs

`results_legacy_eg_check/` holds eight hash-locked JSON files from the August
2026 legacy-EG check (CAFE-In/Pre on Ego-FB, Oklahoma, UNC; CI-Pre and MB-Post
on Pokec-n).  They are retained unchanged for `make verify-certified`.  The
paper's Pokec-n CI-Pre and MB-Post rows were later re-evaluated with exact
Closeness; their evidence is in `evidence/`.
